The first call is thirty minutes, free, and structured. Here is what we cover and how to prepare.

## What we discuss

We start with where you are now: country of residence, current status if you are already in Canada, family situation, work history, education. Then we talk about what you want: stay long-term, study and stay, sponsor a partner, bring your parents, hire a foreign worker. Then we map the realistic paths between those two points.

By the end of the call you will know:

- Which programs are realistic for you.
- Which are not, and why.
- What your next move is, with or without my help.
- What working with me would look like, including fees.

## How to prepare

Two short lists make the call far more productive.

The "current state" list:

- Country of citizenship and country of current residence.
- Current immigration status, if you are in Canada.
- Last completed education and where.
- Last three jobs with role and country.
- Family members involved, if any.

The "what you want" list:

- One or two sentences on the outcome you want.
- Any timing pressure (school start, expiring permit, family event).
- Any constraints (cannot leave current job, cannot move provinces, language preference).

If you have the documents handy, bookmark them, but you do not need to share them on the first call.

## What we do not do on the first call

We do not give you a CRS score number on the spot. We do not write opinions on probability of refusal without seeing the file. We do not promise outcomes. Those come after we see the actual evidence.

If after the call you want to keep working together, we agree on scope and a flat fee in writing before any work begins.
