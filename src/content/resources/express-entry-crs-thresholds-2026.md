The Comprehensive Ranking System scores in early 2026 have continued the pattern of category-based draws. General draws remain less frequent than category-specific ones for healthcare, trades, and French-speaking candidates.

## What this means in practice

If your CRS sits in the low 470s, the path to an Invitation to Apply is more likely through a category match than a general draw. Look at the published categories carefully. A small change in language test results, a French test, or completion of an in-Canada certification can move you into eligibility for a category.

## When to reassess

Recalculate your CRS after each life change that affects it: a new language test result, a new offer of arranged employment, completion of a Canadian credential, or a province issuing a nomination. Even a modest score gain can push you into the receiving range of a category-based draw.

## Where consultations help

The CRS is a calculator. The judgment calls are about which inputs to invest in next: another language test, a French exam, a credential evaluation, a job search in a province with active nominations. We can sit down and prioritize.
