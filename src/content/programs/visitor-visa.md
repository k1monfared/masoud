---
title: Visitor Visa
goal: visit
category: Temporary Resident Visa
order: 1
summary: Temporary entry for tourism, family visits, or business meetings.
processingTime: A few weeks to a few months depending on country and time of year.
languageRequirement: None.
governmentFeeRange: CAD 100 single or multiple entry, plus CAD 85 biometrics where applicable.
eligibilityBullets:
  - Valid passport from a country that requires a Temporary Resident Visa.
  - Sufficient funds for the visit.
  - Genuine purpose of visit and ties to home country.
  - No inadmissibility on health, criminality, or security grounds.
faqs:
  - q: How long can I stay on a visitor visa?
    a: Officers usually authorize stays up to 6 months at a time. The exact length is set on entry by the Canada Border Services Agency officer.
  - q: Can I work or study on a visitor visa?
    a: Generally no. Short courses under 6 months may be allowed. Work requires a separate authorization.
  - q: I have an eTA. Why do I need a visitor visa?
    a: An eTA covers visa-exempt nationalities arriving by air. If you are from a visa-required country, or arriving by land or sea, you need a visa.
  - q: Can I extend my stay?
    a: Yes, by applying for a Visitor Record before your authorized stay ends. The extension is a separate application.
