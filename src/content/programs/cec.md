faqs:
  - q: Do I need a job offer to apply through CEC?
    a: No. CEC does not require a job offer. A valid offer can add CRS points but is not an eligibility requirement.
  - q: Does part-time experience count?
    a: Yes, if it adds up to the equivalent of 1 year of full-time work, meaning 1,560 hours over up to 3 years. Multiple part-time roles can be combined.
  - q: I worked in Canada on a co-op or internship. Does that count?
    a: No. Work experience earned during full-time studies, including co-op terms, does not count toward CEC.
  - q: What if my CRS score is below the typical cut-off?
    a: Build the score you can. Improve language test results, complete a Canadian credential evaluation if relevant, and watch for category-based draws that may invite at lower scores.

## Who it is for

People who already worked in Canada in a skilled occupation and want to apply for permanent residence.

## Key requirements

At least one year of full-time skilled work experience in Canada within the last three years, plus the language results required for the National Occupational Classification level of that work.

## Process overview

Submit an Express Entry profile, receive an Invitation to Apply when your score qualifies, then submit a complete application.
