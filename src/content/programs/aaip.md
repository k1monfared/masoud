## In plain English

Alberta wants to grow its workforce and attract people with skills the provincial economy needs. The AAIP is how it does that. If you are already working in Alberta and your employer wants to keep you permanently, the Alberta Opportunity Stream is built for exactly that situation. If you are not in Alberta yet but you are in the federal Express Entry pool with a background that matches Alberta's needs, the province might reach out to you directly. Either way, a nomination from Alberta puts you on a fast track to permanent residence.

## Who this is for

AAIP is for internationally trained workers who are either already in Alberta on a work permit or who have skills in fields Alberta is actively recruiting, including healthcare, technology, trades, and agriculture. There are also streams for rural communities looking to attract workers, and for entrepreneurs who want to build a business in the province. If you have tourism or hospitality experience, there is a dedicated stream for that too.

## What you need

For the worker streams, the key requirements are a genuine job offer from an Alberta employer, work experience in the relevant occupation, a wage that meets or exceeds the stream minimum, and a language test result at the required level. The employer must also meet Alberta's eligibility requirements, so the job offer alone is not enough. For entrepreneur streams, you need a credible business plan, personal net worth above the program threshold, and relevant business experience.

## How the process works

1. Confirm which stream applies to your situation. Use the Alberta government's program pages to check eligibility criteria.
2. Gather your documents: job offer letter, proof of experience, language test results, and identity documents.
3. Submit your application online through the AAIP portal.
4. Alberta reviews your file. They may request additional information.
5. If approved, you receive a nomination certificate.
6. Apply to IRCC for permanent residence within the timeframe stated on your certificate.

## Things people get wrong

- Accepting a job offer from an employer who does not meet AAIP's employer requirements. The offer must come from a genuinely established Alberta business with the right credentials.
- Applying to the Opportunity Stream while still outside Alberta. This stream is for people already working in the province on an active permit.
- Providing language test results that are below the threshold for your specific occupation category. Different NOC TEER levels have different minimums.
- Underestimating how long the federal PR step takes after nomination. Budget for additional months of processing by IRCC.
