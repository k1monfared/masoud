title: Study Permit
goal: study
category: Study
order: 1
summary: Authorization to attend a designated learning institution in Canada.
processingTime: About 4 to 12 weeks for most countries, longer when biometrics or medicals are needed.
languageRequirement: Set by the institution rather than IRCC. Most programs require IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80, with variation by program.
governmentFeeRange: CAD 150 study permit fee plus biometrics where applicable.
eligibilityBullets:
  - Acceptance at a Designated Learning Institution.
  - Proof of funds covering tuition, living expenses, and return travel.
  - Provincial Attestation Letter where required.
  - Intent to leave Canada at the end of authorized stay, or eligibility for a transition pathway.
faqs:
  - q: Do I need a Provincial Attestation Letter?
    a: Most undergraduate and college applicants do. Some master's, doctoral, and exchange applicants are exempt. Check the rule for your program and province.
  - q: Can I work while studying?
    a: Eligible full-time students can work off-campus up to a set weekly limit during studies, and full-time during scheduled breaks. Conditions appear on your study permit.
  - q: How much money do I need to show?
    a: As of 2026, the threshold for a single student outside Quebec is in the low to mid five figures CAD per year of study, in addition to first-year tuition. Verify the current figure on the IRCC website.
  - q: My application was refused. What now?
    a: Read the refusal carefully. Most refusals can be addressed with a fresh application that targets the officer's specific concern. We can help reshape the file.

A study permit is required for most international students enrolled in programs longer than six months. Eligibility includes acceptance at a designated learning institution and proof of funds.
