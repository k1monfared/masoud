## In plain English

When your partner moves to Canada to study or work, you should not have to sit on the sidelines. The spousal open work permit lets you work for any Canadian employer while your partner is here on their permit. You get real work flexibility, not just a volunteer card. This matters because working in Canada builds your own immigration profile, which gives your family more options when it is time to apply for permanent residence.

## Who this is for

This permit is for spouses and common-law partners of certain international students in graduate and professional programs, and for spouses of foreign workers in skilled occupations. If your partner is here on a study permit for a graduate degree, a law degree, a medical degree, or certain other professional programs, you likely qualify. If your partner is working in a NOC TEER 0 or TEER 1 occupation on a work permit, you likely qualify too. Partners of workers in TEER 2 or TEER 3 occupations may qualify depending on the type of work permit the primary holder has.

## What you need

You need to show that your relationship is genuine through official documents like a marriage certificate, proof of cohabitation, or a statutory declaration for common-law relationships. You need proof that your partner holds a qualifying permit, including a copy of their permit and their Letter of Acceptance or employment record. Your own identity documents and passport are also required.

## How the process works

1. Confirm that your partner's permit type and occupation qualify you for the spousal open work permit.
2. Gather relationship documents and your partner's permit documentation.
3. Apply online through your IRCC account. You can apply at the same time as your partner applies or renews.
4. Provide biometrics at a local visa application centre if required.
5. Receive your open work permit, which will typically be valid for the same period as your partner's permit.

## Things people get wrong

- Applying based on a partner in a college diploma program that does not qualify under the current rules. The eligible study category was narrowed and many spouses were affected.
- Forgetting that your work permit duration matches your partner's, so if your partner has only 6 months left, your permit will also expire then. Renew together.
- Assuming common-law status is automatically accepted. You need to document your cohabitation with proof, not just declare it.
- Not applying soon enough. Processing times mean a gap between your arrival and the permit being issued, during which you may not be able to work.
